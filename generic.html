Hold the Door - AI Tower Defence

Role: AI Programmer | Engine: Unity | Language: C# | Year: 2025

Hold the Door is an autonomous tower-defence simulation developed to explore and compare Rule-Based Systems and Behaviour Trees for game AI. Rather than relying on player input, agents independently evaluate the game state and make tactical decisions.

  • Built a custom Rule-Based System for archer AI using prioritised rules and an arbiter to select actions.
  • Implemented behaviours including target prioritisation, objective defence, counter-attacking, healing, reloading, and enemy selection.
  • Built a custom Behaviour Tree framework using selectors, sequences, conditions, and reusable action nodes.
  • Developed goblin AI capable of seeking cover and healing when injured, attacking strategic objectives, and adapting behaviour based on health and world state.
  • Created ranged enemy AI that manages ammunition, searches for targets, attacks archers, and moves to designated reload locations.
